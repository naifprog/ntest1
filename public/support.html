Cubivo Support

Welcome to the official support page for Cubivo.

How to play

Rotate the cube structure and select available cubes to match three of the same color. Keep the seven-slot collection tray from filling up.

Saving progress

Your progress is stored locally on the device. Settings allows you to export a save code for manual transfer. Keep your save code private.

Hearts

You start with five hearts. Losing a level uses one heart. One heart regenerates every 15 minutes until the balance is full. Rewarded ads are disabled in this release.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, level number, and a description of the problem. Do not publicly share your save code.
